# AKW-100 · 50 Typography

**AKW-50.1** Keynote text is ALL CAPS.

**AKW-50.2** Abbreviate units. Put one space between the number and the unit.

```
3 IN.        1/4 IN.        10 MM        8 FT.
```

**AKW-50.3** Never use the inch mark `"` or the foot mark `'`. They are lost in
CAD fonts, in scanned sets, and in PDF text search, and they collide with quote
marks.

**AKW-50.4** Default to imperial units. Where the prompt or the project is
metric, follow it. Do not convert a unit that belongs to a product's own name:
`10 MM RUBBER FLOORING` stays as the manufacturer sells it.

**AKW-50.5** Underline a product called out by name.

**AKW-50.6** Write a ratio as `1:12`. No spaces, no word "to".

**AKW-50.7** Do not use quote marks for emphasis. In a Keynote a quote mark
reads as an inch mark.

- Defect: `REMOVE EXISTING ELECTRICAL 'FIXTURE' OR 'OUTLET'`
- Fixed: `REMOVE EXISTING LIGHTING FIXTURE OR RECEPTACLE`

**AKW-50.8** Write a fraction as `1/4`, not a decimal and not a typographic
fraction glyph.

**AKW-50.9** A parenthesis must close. An unbalanced parenthesis in an issued
Keynote is a defect, not a typo, because the reader cannot tell where the aside
was meant to end.
