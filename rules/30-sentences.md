# AKW-100 · 30 Sentences

**AKW-30.1** A sentence has 25 words maximum.

**AKW-30.2** A Keynote has 5 sentences and 80 words maximum. A Parent and each
Variant are counted separately.

**AKW-30.3** Over either cap, split the Keynote into a Parent and Variants. The
caps exist to force that split. A note that will not fit is a note doing two
jobs.

**AKW-30.4** Write in the active voice, as an imperative.

- Write: `CLEAN, PATCH, AND REPAIR ALL PARTITIONS.`
- Not: `ALL PARTITIONS SHALL BE CLEANED, PATCHED, AND REPAIRED.`

**AKW-30.5** One idea per sentence. A comma splice is a defect. Two independent
clauses joined by a comma become two sentences.

- Defect: `VENDOR SHALL VERIFY ALL CONDITIONS, IF SCOPE REQUIRES RELOCATION...`
- Fixed: `VENDOR SHALL VERIFY ALL CONDITIONS. IF SCOPE REQUIRES RELOCATION...`

**AKW-30.6** The default Actor is the general contractor, declared once in a
General Note. A Keynote names an Actor **only** when the work is not the general
contractor's: a separate-permit trade, a vendor, an owner-supplied installer.

Naming the general contractor on some notes and not others invites the question
of who performs the unnamed ones.

**AKW-30.7** `SHALL` states a condition the finished work must hold. The
imperative states an action to perform. Prefer the imperative.

- Action: `REPAINT ALL SURFACES TO MATCH EXISTING IN KIND.`
- Condition: `A RISE OVER 3 IN. SHALL BE RAMPED, NOT STEEPER THAN 1:12.`

**AKW-30.8** Do not open a Keynote with a courtesy or a hedge. No `PLEASE NOTE`,
`IT IS INTENDED THAT`, `THE CONTRACTOR IS ADVISED`. Start with the verb or the
subject of the work.
