# AKW-100 · 00 Scope

**AKW-00.1** AKW-100 governs the wording of Keynotes and General Notes on
architectural construction drawings.

**AKW-00.2** AKW-100 does not govern specification sections, schedules, sheet
titles, dimensions, or drawing labels.

**AKW-00.3** A Keynote is a contract document. Where a Keynote conflicts with
another contract document, remove the conflict. Do not rely on an order of
precedence to settle it.

**AKW-00.4** AKW-100 conforms to the U.S. National CAD Standard, Uniform Drawing
System, Module 07 *Notations*, which governs format. AKW-100 governs wording.
Where the two appear to conflict, NCS wins on format and AKW-100 wins on
wording.

**AKW-00.5** Terms used in this standard are defined in `CONTEXT.md`. Use the
canonical term. Terms listed under `_Avoid_` are not permitted in a Keynote or
in this standard.

**AKW-00.6** A rule is cited as `AKW-<area>.<number>`. Example: `AKW-40.3`.

**AKW-00.7** A rule ID is never renumbered and never reused. A rule leaves the
standard in one of two ways, and either way it is recorded in
[`rules/90-archive.md`](./90-archive.md)

A stable ID means a review comment written today still resolves in five years.

**AKW-00.8** Until AKW-100 is first issued, rule IDs may be reorganised freely
and nothing is archived. **At first issue, IDs freeze.**

## What AKW-100 is for

A Keynote is read by a contractor pricing work, a subcontractor building it, and
a lawyer arguing about it. All three read the same words differently. AKW-100
removes the room between those three readings.
